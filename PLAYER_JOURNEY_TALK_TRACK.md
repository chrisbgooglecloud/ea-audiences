## 🎯 Executive Q&A / Objection Handling

### Q1: *"How does this differ from traditional marketing automation (Braze, Salesforce Marketing Cloud)?"*
> **Answer:** Traditional tools rely on static user properties and batch ETL syncs (hours to days late). This architecture operates directly on **Cloud Spanner Property Graph**, evaluating multi-dimensional relationships (clan ties, loss streaks, creator affinities) within sub-second query latency and triggering in-session marketing before the player logs off.

### Q2: *"Can this handle multi-title EA data (e.g. cross-promoting FC 26 players to Apex)?"*
> **Answer:** Yes. The Spanner Graph schema stores unified player identities across FC, Apex, Madden, Battlefield, and The Sims. The journey engine tracks cross-franchise gravity—for instance, identifying when an Apex player goes on a loss streak and cross-promoting a new Rush 5v5 season in FC 26.

### Q3: *"What models power the real-time intelligence?"*
> **Answer:** We use **Vertex AI Gemini 3.5 / 2.5 Flash-Lite**, chosen for sub-500ms latency, high reasoning quality, and low inference cost across millions of automated audience evaluations.
